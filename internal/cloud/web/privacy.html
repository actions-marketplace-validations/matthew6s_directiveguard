Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

DirectiveGuard Cloud is operated by Matthew Searce in the United States. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to info@searcedesigns.com.

Information we collect

  • GitHub account identifiers, username, available email address, and avatar when you sign in.
  • Project names, API-key prefixes and hashes, plan and billing identifiers.
  • Scan metadata, including commit and branch identifiers, file paths, rule findings, severity, descriptions, and remediation text.
  • Operational information such as request times, errors, and security logs.

DirectiveGuard does not require repository source code to be uploaded. API-key secrets are displayed once and stored only as hashes. Stripe processes payment-card details; DirectiveGuard does not receive or store complete card numbers.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, provide scan history, enforce plan limits, process subscriptions, secure and troubleshoot the service, prevent abuse, and comply with law. We do not sell personal information or use scan data to train AI models.

Service providers

We share information as needed with GitHub for authentication, Stripe for billing, and infrastructure providers for hosting, networking, logging, and backups. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and security

Information is retained while needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Free-plan dashboard history is limited to 30 days. We use HTTPS, signed secure cookies, hashed API keys, access controls, and restricted infrastructure, but no system is completely secure.

Your choices

You may revoke API keys, disconnect GitHub authorization, cancel a subscription through the billing portal, or request access, correction, export, or deletion by contacting us. Some records may be retained when legally required.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.

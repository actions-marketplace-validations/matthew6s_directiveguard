Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Monthly subscription

DirectiveGuard Team is billed at $29 USD each month and renews automatically until canceled. You can cancel through the dashboard's billing portal. Cancellation stops future renewals and normally takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period, so Team features remain available through that date.

Refunds

Payments are generally non-refundable once a billing period begins, and we do not normally provide prorated refunds for unused time. If you were charged after canceling, experienced a duplicate or incorrect charge, or the service was materially unavailable, contact us within 14 days of the charge. We will review the request and provide a correction or refund when appropriate. Nothing in this policy limits refund rights required by applicable law.

How to request help

Email info@searcedesigns.com from the address associated with your account. Include the charge date and a description, but do not send complete payment-card details.
